Everything a company runs on

For companies that want their tools in one place, in English and Arabic.

✦ AI layer

  • AI Copilot (Ctrl+K) that sees all five modules.
  • Takes actions: creates a lead, a mailbox or a domain.
  • Daily brief and needs attention with one-click fixes.
  • Writes email replies, summaries, follow-ups and website copy.
  • Local and private: runs on your computer through Ollama.

🌐 Domain

  • DNS records generated for email and the website.
  • Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho or the built-in mail server.
  • Live DNS and SSL check, expiry tracking and a registry lookup.
  • Domain search and a zone-file download.

✉️ Company Email

  • A mailbox for every employee and shared team inboxes.
  • AI triage: sales, support, billing, partnership, HR, spam.
  • CRM next to every email, and one click to make a lead.
  • Built-in SMTP server, DKIM-signed outgoing mail and an inbound webhook.

🖥️ Website

  • Page editor with live preview and AI copywriting.
  • Products live from ERP, with price and stock.
  • Contact form → CRM + sales@ + AI reply.
  • Visits, sources, conversion; Arabic right-to-left sites.

👥 CRM

  • Drag-and-drop pipeline with an AI score per deal.
  • One timeline per customer: web, email, AI, invoices.
  • Next best action, stalled-deal warnings and tasks.
  • Companies and an AI follow-up email for any deal.

⚙️ ERP

  • Finance dashboard and receivables aging.
  • Inventory, sales orders, invoices with print/PDF and reminders.
  • Purchasing with auto-reorder and suppliers; expenses.
  • Team: roles, salaries and leave, with a mailbox for every hire.

Good to know: it runs on Windows, macOS or Linux with Node.js, and all data stays in one database file on your machine. Email between colleagues is delivered instantly. To send to other companies, you connect your mail provider's SMTP account. To receive directly, it needs a server with a public IP, or you can use the inbound webhook. Domains are bought at a registrar; the system manages them and writes their DNS records. AI writing needs Ollama with a model installed, and the built-in engine covers the rest. The interface is built for desktop and laptop screens.

About the demo: Atlas Office Solutions, its owner "Layla Mansour", its customers, emails and figures are fictional sample data. The domain atlasoffice.test is a reserved test name, so its DNS records are shown as already published.
